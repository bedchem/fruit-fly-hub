Not a gambling site

Fly Lab is an educational neuroscience simulation. There is no money, no betting, no prizes and nothing to buy: a simulated fly plays alone, and the credits have no value of any kind.

The bar experiment does not promote or sell alcohol or nicotine. It shows a simulated fly drinking, passing out, poisoning itself with nicotine and waking up hungover; nothing on this site is advice about either substance.

The trading desk does not use real markets, real prices or real money. Its ticker, prices and headlines are generated by a seeded simulation, the fly trades paper money, and nothing on this site is investment advice.

The gaming setup shows a simulated fly playing League of Legends, Minecraft, Fortnite, Counter-Strike 2, Red Dead Redemption 2, God of War (2018), God of War Ragnarök and Rainbow Six Siege. Gameplay is drawn procedurally by this site; the Siege lodge uses CC0 material photos from Poly Haven. Its CS2 case-opening simulation uses locally hosted skin images credited to Valve and contributing artists. Drops are fictional, with no purchases, monetary value or Steam connection.

If gambling is causing you problems, help is free and anonymous. In Italy, call the national gambling helpline Telefono Verde Nazionale per le problematiche legate al Gioco d'Azzardo on 800 558 822.

Privacy policy (Informativa sulla privacy, art. 13 GDPR)

Who is responsible

The data controller is %SITE.operator.name%, reachable at %SITE.operator.email%.

What this site does not do

  • This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage for tracking.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels.
  • No third-party requests by default: fonts, 3D models and connectome data are all served from this site. The only exception is optional and off until you switch it on: TikTok videos in the doomscroll experiment (see below).
  • The simulations run entirely in your browser. Nothing about what you watch or click is sent to us.

That is why there is no site-wide cookie banner. You are asked only on the one page where TikTok content can appear, and only if you want to see it.

TikTok videos in the doomscroll experiment (only with your consent)

The doomscroll experiment can show real TikTok videos (fan edits about %SITE.editSubject%) through TikTok's official embedded player, and a live feed of the hashtag #%SITE.editTag% through TikTok's official hashtag embed. Until you choose Allow TikTok videos, nothing is loaded from TikTok: no player, no feed, no script, no connection. The experiment works fully without it.

Which videos the flies can scroll comes from a list published on this site, kept up to date from public web search results and TikTok's public oEmbed service; loading that list sends nothing to TikTok. So that there is no pause between videos, each phone loads its next video in advance, muted and paused.

If you allow it, your browser connects directly to TikTok's servers when a video or the hashtag feed is shown or loaded in advance. TikTok then receives technical data such as your IP address, browser and device information and the address of this page, and TikTok may store or read cookies and similar information on your device, including for its own analytics and advertising. What TikTok does with these data is decided by TikTok: see TikTok's privacy policy and cookie policy. The provider for users in the European Economic Area is TikTok Technology Limited, 10 Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin D02 T380, Ireland. TikTok may transfer data to countries outside the EU.

  • Legal basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading information on your device, art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code implementing art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive).
  • Who is responsible: for making the connection to TikTok by embedding the player and the hashtag feed, we and TikTok may be jointly responsible (see CJEU, C-40/17 Fashion ID). For everything TikTok does with the data afterwards, TikTok alone is responsible. You can exercise your rights with us or with TikTok.
  • Withdrawing: you can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, with the button below or with Cookie settings on the experiment page. Players and the hashtag feed are removed immediately. Cookies TikTok has already set are under TikTok's control; you can delete them in your browser settings. Withdrawing does not affect what happened before.
  • Your choice itself is saved in your browser's local storage under the key flylab.consent: only the answer and when you gave it. This is strictly necessary to respect your choice and is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • The list of videos: while TikTok is allowed, the last list of TikTok posts the page loaded from this site is kept in your browser's local storage under the key flylab.edits, so the videos start without waiting on your next visit. It holds only public post addresses and creators' names, nothing about you, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you withdraw your consent.

Your choice can be changed here once JavaScript is available.

Simulation inventory

The gaming experiment saves collected simulation skins on your device under the local-storage key flylab.cs2.inventory.v1, so your inventory survives a reload. It contains only fictional drops and their appearance attributes, and is never sent to us or any third party. Clearing site data removes it. If browser storage is unavailable, the inventory lasts only for the current visit. Skin images are served from this site without requests to Valve, Steam or GitHub.

Server logs

This processing is necessary to operate and secure the site (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest).

The website and its server infrastructure are operated by %SITE.operator.name%. These technical data are not used to identify you and are not combined with other data. Server logs are retained only for as long as necessary for security, troubleshooting and the reliable operation of the website.
